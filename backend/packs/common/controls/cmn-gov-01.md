## Objective
Agreed management actions are tracked, validated and closed on time.

## Test of design
- Inspect the action-tracking process and reporting to the audit committee.

## Test of effectiveness
- Select closed actions and verify evidence of remediation; select open actions and assess overdue status and escalation.

## Checklist
- Are overdue actions escalated?
- Is closure supported by evidence?

## Recommendation
Validate closure evidence before closing actions and report overdue actions to the audit committee.
