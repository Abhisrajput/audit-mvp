## Objective
Hedge relationships are formally designated and documented at inception so that hedge accounting is supportable.

## Test of design
- Inspect the hedge documentation template for objective, hedged item, instrument, hedge ratio and effectiveness method.

## Test of effectiveness
- For a sample of new hedge relationships confirm documentation was completed on or before the designation date.

## Checklist
- Was documentation in place at inception for each hedge?

## Recommendation
Complete hedge documentation on or before designation and review it monthly with accounting.
