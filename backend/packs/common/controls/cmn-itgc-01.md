## Objective
Access to ERP and quality systems is limited to users with a current business need.

## Test of design
- Inspect the access review procedure for scope (systems, privileged roles) and reviewer independence.

## Test of effectiveness
- For sampled quarters inspect evidence that business owners reviewed complete user lists and that removals were actioned timely.

## Checklist
- Were all in-scope systems reviewed each quarter?
- Were leavers removed within the policy timeline?

## Recommendation
Complete quarterly access reviews for all in-scope systems and track removals to closure.
