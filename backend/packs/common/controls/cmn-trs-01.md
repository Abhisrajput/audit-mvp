## Objective
Commodity, FX and interest-rate exposures are hedged within a board-approved policy and limit framework.

## Test of design
- Inspect the hedging policy for approval, review date, authorised instruments, tenor and volume limits, and delegations of authority.

## Test of effectiveness
- Confirm the current policy was approved by the board or its committee and that limits in trading systems agree to the policy.

## Checklist
- Is the policy approved and reviewed within the last 12 months?
- Do system limits match policy limits?

## Recommendation
Re-approve the hedging policy annually and reconcile system limits to the approved policy.
