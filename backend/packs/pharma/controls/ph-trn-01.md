## Objective
Staff are trained on cGMP and on the SOPs for their role before performing tasks.

## Test of design
- Inspect the training procedure, role-based curricula and qualification of trainers.

## Test of effectiveness
- For sampled staff who performed batch steps confirm training on the relevant SOP version was completed before the task date.

## Checklist
- Was training complete before task performance?
- Is annual GMP refresher training current?

## Recommendation
Link role curricula to SOP versions and block task assignment until training is complete.
