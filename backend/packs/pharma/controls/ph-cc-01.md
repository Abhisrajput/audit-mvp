## Objective
GMP-relevant changes are assessed, approved and verified before and after implementation.

## Test of design
- Inspect the change control SOP for risk assessment, approvals and post-implementation review.

## Test of effectiveness
- For sampled changes confirm QA approval before implementation and post-implementation effectiveness review.

## Checklist
- Was QA approval obtained before implementation?
- Was post-implementation review completed?

## Recommendation
Ensure every GMP change has QA pre-approval and a post-implementation review.
