## Objective
Every deviation is documented, root-caused and assessed for impact on other batches.

## Test of design
- Inspect the deviation procedure for classification, timelines, root cause tools and batch impact assessment.

## Test of effectiveness
- For sampled deviations confirm timely closure, documented root cause, and impact extended to other potentially affected batches.

## Checklist
- Were investigations closed within the SOP timeline?
- Was impact on other batches assessed?
- Is root cause supported by evidence?

## Recommendation
Strengthen root cause analysis and require documented batch impact assessment for every deviation.
