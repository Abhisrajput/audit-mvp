## Objective
Laboratory data are complete and out-of-specification results are investigated.

## Test of design
- Inspect the OOS procedure and laboratory documentation practices.

## Test of effectiveness
- For sampled tests confirm complete raw data, analyst and reviewer signatures; for sampled OOS confirm phase I/II investigations were completed and justified.

## Checklist
- Is raw data complete?
- Were OOS results invalidated only with a scientifically justified root cause?

## Recommendation
Require scientifically justified root cause before invalidating any OOS result and trend OOS by product and test.
