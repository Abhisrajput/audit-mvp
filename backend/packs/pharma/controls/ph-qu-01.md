## Objective
The quality unit independently approves or rejects materials, procedures and batches.

## Test of design
- Inspect the quality manual and organisation chart for QU independence and written responsibilities.

## Test of effectiveness
- Confirm sampled batch releases, SOP approvals and specification changes were approved by the QU.

## Checklist
- Is the QU independent of production?
- Are QU responsibilities written and current?

## Recommendation
Document QU responsibilities and ensure all releases and GMP document approvals carry QU sign-off.
