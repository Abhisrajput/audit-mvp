## Objective
Equipment is cleaned and maintained per schedule with contemporaneous logbook entries.

## Test of design
- Inspect cleaning SOPs, cleaning validation and preventive maintenance schedule.

## Test of effectiveness
- For sampled equipment and batches confirm logbook entries for cleaning and maintenance are complete and timely.

## Checklist
- Are logbook entries contemporaneous and signed?
- Is preventive maintenance on schedule?

## Recommendation
Enforce contemporaneous logbook entries and track preventive maintenance overdue items.
