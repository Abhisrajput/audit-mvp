## Objective
Components come from qualified suppliers; reliance on COAs is supported by verification testing.

## Test of design
- Inspect the vendor qualification SOP, approved vendor list and COA reliance criteria.

## Test of effectiveness
- For sampled component lots confirm supplier was approved, identity testing performed, and periodic full testing done where COAs are relied on.

## Checklist
- Was the supplier approved before use?
- Is identity testing performed on every lot?

## Recommendation
Qualify suppliers before use and periodically verify COA reliability by full testing.
