## Objective
Self-inspections cover all GMP areas on a risk basis and observations are closed.

## Test of design
- Inspect the self-inspection plan for coverage, frequency and auditor independence.

## Test of effectiveness
- Confirm planned self-inspections were completed and observations closed with CAPA.

## Checklist
- Did the plan cover all GMP areas?
- Are observations closed?

## Recommendation
Complete the risk-based self-inspection plan and track observations through CAPA to closure.
