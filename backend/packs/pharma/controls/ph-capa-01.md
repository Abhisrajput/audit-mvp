## Objective
Corrective and preventive actions are implemented and verified as effective.

## Test of design
- Inspect the CAPA procedure for effectiveness-check criteria and timing.

## Test of effectiveness
- For sampled closed CAPAs confirm effectiveness checks were performed against defined criteria; check for recurrence.

## Checklist
- Are effectiveness criteria defined up front?
- Has the issue recurred after closure?

## Recommendation
Define measurable effectiveness criteria for every CAPA and verify them before closure.
