## Objective
Product complaints are logged, assessed and investigated with CAPA where needed.

## Test of design
- Inspect the complaint handling SOP for timelines, QU review and trending.

## Test of effectiveness
- For sampled complaints confirm QU review, investigation where warranted and closure within timelines.

## Checklist
- Is every complaint reviewed by QU?
- Are complaints trended?

## Recommendation
Ensure QU review of all complaints and trend them by product and defect type.
