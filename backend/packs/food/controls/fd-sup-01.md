## Objective
Raw materials come only from approved suppliers whose risk is assessed and verified.

## Test of design
- Inspect the supplier approval procedure, raw-material risk assessment and approved supplier list.

## Test of effectiveness
- Select purchases in the period and confirm the supplier was approved before receipt and verification activities (audit, COA, testing) were current.

## Checklist
- Was each supplier approved before first purchase?
- Are high-risk materials tested or audited per plan?

## Recommendation
Block purchasing from unapproved suppliers in the ERP and schedule verification by raw-material risk.
