## Objective
All significant hazards for each product and process step are identified and assessed.

## Test of design
- Inspect the hazard analysis for coverage of all products, process steps, and biological, chemical (incl. allergen) and physical hazards.
- Confirm it is approved by the food safety team / PCQI.

## Test of effectiveness
- Confirm the hazard analysis was reviewed in the last 12 months and after each process or product change in the period.

## Checklist
- Is the process flow verified on the floor?
- Are allergens and foreign-body hazards included?
- Was the analysis reviewed after changes?

## Recommendation
Review the hazard analysis at least annually and on every product or process change, with sign-off by the food safety team / PCQI.
