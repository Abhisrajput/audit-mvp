## Objective
Monitoring and corrective action records are reviewed by a qualified person within the required time.

## Test of design
- Inspect the verification schedule and responsibility for record review.

## Test of effectiveness
- For sampled weeks confirm records were reviewed and signed within 7 working days.

## Checklist
- Is record review within 7 working days?
- Are calibrations of CCP instruments current?

## Recommendation
Track record review timeliness and escalate reviews overdue beyond 7 working days.
