## Objective
The recall procedure works and the team can execute it in time.

## Test of design
- Inspect the recall procedure, team, contacts and regulator notification steps.

## Test of effectiveness
- Inspect the latest mock recall: scope, time taken, percentage recovered and actions raised.

## Checklist
- Was a mock recall done in the last 12 months?
- Were time and recovery targets met?

## Recommendation
Run mock recalls at least annually with defined time and recovery targets and close resulting actions.
