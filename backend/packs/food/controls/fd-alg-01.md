## Objective
Allergens are segregated, cleaned down and correctly declared on labels.

## Test of design
- Inspect the allergen matrix, segregation rules, validated changeover cleaning and label approval process.

## Test of effectiveness
- For sampled allergen changeovers confirm cleaning verification; for sampled products agree label declarations to the recipe and allergen matrix.

## Checklist
- Is cleaning between allergen and non-allergen runs verified?
- Do labels declare all allergens in the recipe?

## Recommendation
Verify allergen changeover cleaning and reconcile labels to recipes before every new label goes live.
