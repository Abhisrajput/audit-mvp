## Objective
Outsourced processing sites meet the company's food safety requirements.

## Test of design
- Inspect the co-manufacturer list, risk rating and audit plan.

## Test of effectiveness
- For sampled co-manufacturers confirm audits or GFSI certificates are current and findings are closed.

## Checklist
- Is every co-manufacturer audited or certified?
- Are audit findings closed?

## Recommendation
Maintain a risk-based co-manufacturer audit plan and track findings to closure.
