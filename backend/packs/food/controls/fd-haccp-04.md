## Objective
Deviations from critical limits trigger documented corrective action and product disposition.

## Test of design
- Inspect the corrective action procedure for product hold, evaluation, disposition and root cause.

## Test of effectiveness
- For all deviations in the period inspect corrective action records, product hold and disposition decisions.

## Checklist
- Was affected product held and evaluated?
- Is disposition approved by QA?

## Recommendation
Ensure every CCP deviation is logged with product hold, QA-approved disposition and root cause.
