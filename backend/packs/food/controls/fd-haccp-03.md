## Objective
Every CCP is monitored at the defined frequency and records are complete, contemporaneous and signed.

## Test of design
- Inspect monitoring procedures for frequency, method and responsibility for each CCP.

## Test of effectiveness
- Select a sample of batch / shift monitoring records and check completeness, readings within limits, and monitor signature.

## Checklist
- Are all readings recorded at the defined frequency?
- Is each record signed by the person monitoring?
- Are out-of-limit readings linked to a corrective action?

## Recommendation
Reinforce contemporaneous recording and signature of CCP monitoring and introduce supervisor end-of-shift checks.
