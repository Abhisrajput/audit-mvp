## Objective
Pest activity is prevented, detected and acted on.

## Test of design
- Inspect the pest control contract, site map of devices and inspection frequency.

## Test of effectiveness
- For sampled months inspect contractor reports, trend analysis and closure of recommendations.

## Checklist
- Are inspections at the contracted frequency?
- Are recommendations closed?

## Recommendation
Trend pest activity monthly and close contractor recommendations within agreed timelines.
