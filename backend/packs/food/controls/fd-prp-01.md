## Objective
Plant and equipment are kept clean and sanitary.

## Test of design
- Inspect the master cleaning schedule and verification methods (visual, ATP, swabs).

## Test of effectiveness
- For sampled days confirm cleaning records are complete and verification results within limits.

## Checklist
- Are cleaning records complete?
- Are failed swabs re-cleaned and re-tested?

## Recommendation
Verify cleaning effectiveness routinely and escalate repeat failures.
