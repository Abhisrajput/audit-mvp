## Objective
Each CCP critical limit is supported by validation evidence.

## Test of design
- Inspect validation studies or scientific references for each CCP critical limit (e.g. roasting time/temperature, metal detector sensitivity).

## Test of effectiveness
- For each CCP confirm validation is current and covers the equipment and products in use.

## Checklist
- Does every CCP have documented validation?
- Is validation repeated after equipment changes?

## Recommendation
Document validation for every critical limit and revalidate on equipment, recipe or process change.
